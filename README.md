# iService  
### Redefining How People Connect With Trusted Artisans  

---

## 🌍 Market Opportunity  
- **$600B+ global home services market**, rapidly expanding with urbanization and digital adoption.  
- In Nigeria alone, the informal artisan sector employs **millions**, yet lacks digital structure, trust, and efficient matching systems.  
- Rising middle-class demand for **on-demand services** creates a massive growth opportunity.  

---

## ❌ The Problem  
- **Customers**: struggle to find **reliable, verified artisans** quickly.  
- **Artisans**: lack consistent jobs, secure payments, and a way to showcase credibility.  
- Reliance on informal word-of-mouth networks results in **untrustworthy, slow, and inefficient experiences**.  

---

## ✅ Our Solution — *iService*  
A **scalable digital marketplace** that connects customers with verified artisans in minutes.  

- ⚡ **Fast Discovery** – Find skilled artisans instantly by category & location.  
- 🔒 **Trust & Verification** – Background checks, ratings, and reviews ensure reliability.  
- 💼 **Fair Work Access** – Artisans gain consistent jobs, secure payments, and reputation growth.  
- 📱 **Seamless Experience** – Easy booking, digital contracts, and transparent pricing.  

---

## 🚀 Why iService Wins  
- **Two-sided marketplace** solving pain points for both customers and artisans.  
- **Mobile-first, built for scale** — speed and simplicity at the core.  
- **Recurring demand** across multiple service categories (plumbing, electrical, cleaning, carpentry, etc.).  
- **Network effect**: more artisans = faster matching = happier customers.  

---

## 📈 Traction & Early Validation  
- Strong urban demand for trusted, on-demand artisans.  
- User surveys show **80%+ frustration** with current methods of finding artisans.  
- Early partnerships & pilot testing with artisan groups in Nigeria.  
- Clear pathway for **regional expansion across Africa**.  

---

## 💡 Vision  
To become the **#1 trusted digital marketplace for services in Africa**, empowering millions of artisans with sustainable livelihoods while giving customers reliable, convenient service access.
